Welcome

This app is designed for kids and families. It uses an AI helper named Robo. For children under 13, a parent or guardian should set it up.

Privacy: This demo stores only a random session ID and your chosen settings in your browser (sessionStorage). No camera use. No face scanning. No personal details required.
Tip: Parent gate uses a quick math question. Then you choose the age tier.

What Robo is

Robo is a computer helper in a cartoon robot body. Robo can help with homework, stories, science facts, feelings, and fun puzzles.

Safety: Robo should not be used for emergencies. If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number.
Kid rule: Please do not share real names, addresses, phone numbers, school names, or passwords.
